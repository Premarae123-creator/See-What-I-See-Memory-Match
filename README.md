# See What I See: Memory Match v9

I SEE MONEY redesign:
- I See Money remains its own mode.
- Number selection now happens BEFORE gameplay in the multiplayer lobby.
- Each player selects one or more available numbers from 1–32 and presses LOCK IN NUMBERS.
- Locked numbers cannot be taken by another player.
- Host START GAME remains disabled until every player in the room has locked at least one number.
- Once the game begins, the 32 cards are shuffled and displayed.
- Players no longer click cards during the Money round.
- Cards automatically flip/reveal in a randomized server-synchronized order.
- Wrong cards retain the shake/fall animation.
- When the $$ card reveals, the player who locked that number wins and gets the winner animation.
- A sticky side chart beside the board shows every player and their locked numbers throughout the round.
- I See Money has NO end-of-game leaderboard; it shows the winner/money number and same-room Play Again controls.
- Same-room Play Again resets the locked numbers so everyone can choose again.
- Camera/mic v8 WebRTC improvements remain.
